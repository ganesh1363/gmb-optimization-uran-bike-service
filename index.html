Project Overview

The project focused on creating, completing and optimizing a Google Business Profile for a local two-wheeler service and spare-parts business.

Project Objective

The objective was to improve the business's local search presence by optimizing business information, researching relevant local keywords, improving services and visual content, and analysing Google Business Profile performance data.

Project Scope

The work covered Google Business Profile setup, business information optimization, local keyword research, service organization, photo content, NAP consistency and performance insights analysis.

Key Findings

Major observations derived from the profile review and Google Business Profile Insights.

Finding 01

The profile has established a verified local presence and is discoverable through Google Search and Google Maps.

Finding 02

Direction requests are a strong practical indicator that users are finding the business and considering a visit.

Finding 03

The profile has limited measurable conversion actions in the exported periods, creating an opportunity to improve customer calls and enquiries.

Finding 04

Continued optimization of photos, services, reviews and business information can help strengthen local visibility and customer confidence.

Recommendations

Suggested actions for maintaining and improving the Google Business Profile after the project.

HIGH PRIORITY

Continue Adding Photos

Upload authentic photos of the shop, services, spare parts and completed work regularly.

HIGH PRIORITY

Encourage Reviews

Encourage satisfied customers to leave genuine reviews and respond professionally to customer feedback.

MEDIUM PRIORITY

Maintain Services

Keep the service list updated whenever new services or repair offerings are introduced.

MEDIUM PRIORITY

Post Regularly

Publish useful Google Business Profile posts about services, offers, maintenance tips and updates.

MEDIUM PRIORITY

Monitor Insights

Review Search, Maps, direction requests and other available customer actions periodically.

ONGOING

Maintain NAP Consistency

Keep the business name, address and phone information consistent across online listings and directories.
