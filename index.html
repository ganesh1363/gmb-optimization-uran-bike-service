Local SEO Project Overview Optimization Insights Findings Google Business Profile • Local SEO GMB Optimization for Local SEO A practical local-search optimization project for Sawant Autogarage and spare parts, a two-wheeler service and spare-parts business in Uran, Navi Mumbai. Project completed as part of the Unified Mentor project assignment • 2026 Project Overview The project focused on creating, completing and optimizing a Google Business Profile, researching local keywords, improving business information, reviewing local citations, and analysing Google Business Profile performance data. Business Sawant Autogarage and spare parts Category Motorcycle shop Location Uran, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra Status Verified Google Business Profile Project Objectives 01 · GMB Setup Create and verify a Google Business Profile and ensure core business details are complete. 02 · Keyword Research Identify relevant local search phrases connected with bike servicing, repairs and spare parts. 03 · Content Optimization Improve the business description, services and visual content to make the listing more useful to local customers. 04 · Local Citations Review Name, Address and Phone consistency across online listings and directories. 05 · Insights Analysis Use Google Business Profile performance data to understand visibility and customer actions. Optimization Work Completed The profile was reviewed and optimized around the information visible to local customers. Business Description Added a detailed description covering two-wheeler servicing, engine repair, oil changes, battery replacement, tyre and puncture repair, brake maintenance and spare parts. Services Service offerings were organized around common customer requirements and local two-wheeler maintenance needs. Photos Real business photos were added to improve authenticity and help customers recognize the physical location. NAP Information Business name, address and phone information were reviewed for consistency on the Google profile. Local Keyword Focus The keyword strategy focused on high-intent local searches rather than broad generic terms. Calls, messages, bookings and website clicks were recorded as zero in the supplied exports. Key Findings & Recommendations Finding 1 The profile has established a verified local presence and is discoverable through Google Search and Maps. Finding 2 Direction requests are a strong practical indicator that users are finding the business and considering a visit. Finding 3 The profile has limited measurable conversion actions in the exported periods, creating an opportunity to improve customer calls and enquiries. Recommendation Continue adding genuine photos, encourage satisfied customers to leave reviews, keep services updated, publish regular posts and maintain consistent NAP information.
