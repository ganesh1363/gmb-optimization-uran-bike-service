Google Business Profile Optimization

A Local SEO Optimization Project for Sawant Autogarage and Spare Parts

Academic Project • Unified Mentor • 2026

Project Overview

This project focused on creating, completing and optimizing a Google Business Profile for Sawant Autogarage and Spare Parts, a two-wheeler service and spare-parts business located in Uran, Navi Mumbai.


The project applied local SEO principles including business information optimization, local keyword research, service optimization, visual content improvement, NAP consistency and Google Business Profile Insights analysis.

Project Objectives

  • Create and verify the Google Business Profile.
  • Improve the business description and service information.
  • Identify relevant local search keywords.
  • Improve the visual presentation using real business photographs.
  • Review business name, address and phone information for consistency.
  • Analyse Google Business Profile Insights.
  • Develop recommendations for improving local visibility and customer actions.

Optimization Work Completed

Business Description

Added a detailed description covering two-wheeler servicing, engine repair, oil changes, battery replacement, tyre and puncture repair, brake maintenance and spare parts.

Services

Organized service offerings around common customer requirements and local two-wheeler maintenance needs.

Photos

Added real business photographs to improve authenticity and help customers recognize the physical location.

NAP Information

Reviewed the business name, address and phone information for consistency across the Google Business Profile.

Local Keyword Research

The keyword strategy focused on high-intent local searches related to motorcycle servicing, repairs and spare parts.

Key Findings

Finding 1: Verified Local Presence

The business has established a verified local presence and is discoverable through Google Search and Google Maps.

Finding 2: Strong Direction Requests

Direction requests indicate that users are finding the business and considering a physical visit.

Finding 3: Opportunity for More Customer Actions

The profile has limited measurable conversion actions in the exported periods, creating an opportunity to improve customer calls, enquiries and reviews.

Recommendations

  • Continue adding genuine business photographs regularly.
  • Encourage satisfied customers to leave Google reviews.
  • Keep service information updated.
  • Maintain consistent business name, address and phone information.
  • Publish regular Google Business Profile posts.
  • Monitor Google Business Profile Insights regularly.
  • Focus on high-intent local keywords related to bike servicing and repairs.

Conclusion

The Google Business Profile optimization project helped establish and improve the online local presence of Sawant Autogarage and Spare Parts. The optimization focused on accurate business information, relevant local keywords, service information, authentic photographs and performance analysis.


The Insights data indicates that the profile is receiving visibility through Google Search and Maps, while direction requests demonstrate meaningful local customer interest. Continued content updates, reviews and profile activity can further strengthen local visibility and customer engagement.
